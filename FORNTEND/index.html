    About

    SpecCipher

    Standards recommendation for procurement specifications.

    What it does

    SpecCipher helps identify potentially applicable Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) specifications for a procurement requirement. Provide a description of what you are procuring and review the ranked standards returned by the system.

    How to use it

    • Describe the product, requirement, application, or operating conditions.
    • Attach a tender or specification PDF when needed.
    • Select Analyze and review the results.
    • Open a result to inspect its scope, reasoning, evidence, and related standards.

    Limitations

    SpecCipher is a decision-support tool only. Results are indicative and should be verified against the current official BIS publication before use in tender documents, procurement contracts, or compliance decisions.

    SpecCipher is not affiliated with or operated by the Bureau of Indian Standards.
