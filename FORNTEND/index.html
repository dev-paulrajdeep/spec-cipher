    About

    SpecCipher

    Standards recommendation for procurement specifications.

    What it does

    SpecCipher helps identify potentially applicable Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) specifications for a given procurement requirement. Provide a description of what you are procuring — the tool returns a ranked list of candidate standards with evidence of why each was matched.

    Who it is for

    Procurement officers, technical evaluation committees, and specification writers who need to quickly assess which Indian Standards may apply to a product or service.

    How to use it

    • Describe the product, technical requirement, or operating conditions in plain language.
    • Optionally attach a tender document or specification PDF.
    • Click Analyze and review the ranked results.
    • Click View Details & Evidence on any result to inspect supporting information.

    Limitations

    SpecCipher is a decision-support tool only. Results are indicative. All recommendations must be independently verified against the current official BIS publication before use in any tender document, procurement contract, or legal compliance context.

    SpecCipher is not affiliated with or operated by the Bureau of Indian Standards.
