About

SpecCipher

Standards recommendation for procurement specifications.

What it does

SpecCipher helps identify potentially relevant Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) specifications for a given procurement requirement. Provide a description of what you are procuring and the tool returns a ranked list of candidate standards based on retrieved document content.

Who it is for

Procurement officers, technical evaluation committees, and specification writers who need to quickly assess which Indian Standards may be relevant to a product or service.

How to use it

  • Describe the product, technical requirement, operating conditions, application, and quantity in plain language.
  • Click Analyze and review the ranked results.
  • Open View Retrieved Text on a result to inspect the supporting content.

Limitations

Current MVP corpus: one independently indexed standard (IS 5120) and one retrieval chunk. SpecCipher does not claim BIS-wide or multi-standard coverage. Related-standard mentions in evidence are provenance facts, not independently ranked recommendations.

SpecCipher is a decision-support tool only . Retrieval similarity does not by itself establish final applicability, compliance, or legal validity. Results must be independently verified against the current official BIS publication before use in any tender document, procurement contract, or legal compliance context. The original source PDF for the current corpus is not present in this repository; provenance is carried from the canonical JSON artifact.

SpecCipher is not affiliated with or operated by the Bureau of Indian Standards.
